FAQ

Questions?
Answered.

Everything you need to know before your first session.

Is AURELIUS open to the public?

No. AURELIUS is an appointment-only, members-only studio. We maintain a limited membership to ensure every client receives undivided attention and complete privacy during their sessions.

What should I expect in my first session?

Your first visit includes a comprehensive movement assessment, body composition scan, goal-setting consultation, and a tailored introductory workout. Plan for 90 minutes. Wear comfortable athletic clothing — we provide everything else.

Can I pause or cancel my membership?

Yes. Memberships can be paused for up to 3 months per year with no penalty. Cancellation requires 30 days written notice. We believe in earning your loyalty through results, not contracts.

Do you offer couples or partner sessions?

We offer semi-private sessions for up to two people at a reduced rate. Both participants receive individualized programming within a shared session. Contact us for semi-private pricing.

What equipment and facilities are available?

Our studio features Technogym and Eleiko equipment, a full recovery suite with infrared sauna and cold plunge, a performance lab, luxury changing rooms with premium amenities, and a private lounge area.

How do I book a consultation?

Fill out the contact form below or call us directly. A member of our team will reach out within 24 hours to schedule a complimentary 30-minute consultation at the studio.
